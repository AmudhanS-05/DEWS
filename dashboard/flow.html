Replay a flood

Replay refuses dates already replayed since the last reset: press Reset first. Replay sends rain first and the gauges 15 s later. Reset stops Faust and the forecaster for 1.5 to 5 minutes and deletes that flood's SIMULATION rows. An email really sent stays on record.
